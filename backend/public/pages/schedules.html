How schedules work
  • 🔁 A schedule repeats daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly or on a custom interval, or happens once.
  • ✅ Create automatically makes the real transaction on the date (your rules run on it). Ask me first puts it in the approval list above, and your balance only changes when you approve.
  • ⏸ Pausing stops everything. When you resume you can skip what you missed (default) or create it.
  • 🛡 The same date is never created twice, even if you pay a bill early or edit the schedule.
